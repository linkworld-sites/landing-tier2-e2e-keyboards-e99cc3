Our Process

Built in four deliberate stages.

We don't rush. A Keystroke Atelier build typically takes 6–8 weeks — time spent ensuring every element is right.

01

Consultation

A 30-minute call to map your typing style, aesthetic preferences, and intended use. We listen before we spec.

02

Specification & Sourcing

We assemble a full bill of materials — case, PCB, plate, stabilisers, switches, and keycaps — with quotes and alternatives.

03

Atelier Build

Hands-on assembly in our workshop. Every solder joint inspected, every switch tested on an oscilloscope, every key seated flush.

04

Quality Audit & Dispatch

72-hour burn-in, audio recording for your review, then shipped in a custom hard case with full documentation.

Switch Selection

Find your feel.

We carry 48 curated switch options across four feel profiles. Not sure? We'll send a sampler board before your build begins.

🟡

Linear

Smooth, consistent keystrokes with no tactile feedback. Preferred by speed typists and gamers. Favourites: Gateron Oil Kings, Boba LTs.

Smooth & Silent
🟢

Tactile

A satisfying bump tells you exactly when the key registers. Preferred by writers and coders. Favourites: Holy Pandas, U4Ts.

Bump & Precise
🔵

Clicky

Tactile bump with an audible click. Classic and expressive. Favourites: Box Navies, Kailh Creams (clicky mod).

Clack & Click
🟣

Silent

Dampened top and bottom-out for near-silent operation. Ideal for shared spaces. Favourites: Topre 45g, Boba U4.

Whisper Quiet
Materials

Great keyboards begin with great materials. We source aircraft-grade aluminum, premium brass weight inserts, and POM plates — each chosen for feel, acoustics, and longevity.
